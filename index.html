🦧 1. Bekantan — Proboscis Monkey ▼

🏷️ Scientific Name

Nasalis larvatus

👀 Physical Characteristics

  • Large and long nose, especially in males.
  • Reddish-brown fur.
  • Long tail.
  • A body adapted to life around trees and water.

🌳 Habitat

  • Mangrove forests
  • Swamps
  • Riverine forests
  • Forest areas around rivers

🐒 Behavior

  • Lives in groups.
  • Spends much time around trees.
  • Can swim well.
  • Often stays around rivers and mangrove areas.

🍃 Diet

Examples of food include leaves, fruits, and seeds.

⚠️ Threats

  • Habitat loss
  • Deforestation
  • Human activities that change forest areas

🦧 2. Orangutan & Gorilla ▼

🦧 Orangutan

Physical Characteristics

  • Long arms.
  • Strong body.
  • Long hair/fur.
  • Hands and feet adapted for movement in trees.

Habitat

Rainforests and tropical forest areas.

Behavior

  • Spends much time in trees.
  • Uses arms and hands to move between trees.
  • Known as an intelligent animal.

Diet

Fruits, leaves, plants, and insects.

Threats

Habitat loss, deforestation, and destruction of forest areas.

🦍 Gorilla

Physical Characteristics

  • Large body.
  • Strong body.
  • Long arms.
  • Thick fur.

Habitat

Forests and rainforests.

Behavior

  • Lives in groups.
  • Has social behavior within the group.

Diet

Mainly plants such as leaves, stems, and fruits.

Threats

Habitat loss, deforestation, and human activities.

🐦 3. Indonesian Birds — Cendrawasih ▼

👀 Physical Characteristics

🐦 Behavior

🧩 4. Noun Group ▼

A noun group is a group of words whose main word is a noun.

Determiner + Adjective + Noun
a beautiful Indonesian bird

a = determiner
beautiful = adjective
Indonesian = adjective
bird = noun
the endangered Indonesian primate
Main noun → primate
🔄 5. Passive Voice ▼
Subject + TO BE + Past Participle (V3)
Active:
People protect Indonesian birds.

Passive:
Indonesian birds are protected by people.

🔎 Ciri Passive Voice

  • Uses to be.
  • Uses past participle / V3.
  • Can use by to show the doer.

✍️ Report Writing Steps

Gathering → Organizing → Drafting → Revising → Publishing

Gathering = mengumpulkan informasi
Organizing = mengatur informasi
Drafting = membuat rancangan
Revising = memperbaiki
Publishing = mempublikasikan

🏞️ 7. Simple Past Tense ▼

Simple past is used for events that happened in the past.

POSITIVE
S + V2

NEGATIVE
S + did not + V1

QUESTION
Did + S + V1?
I visited the national park.

I did not visit the national park.

Did you visit the national park?

⚠️ Important

After did / didn't, use V1.

❌ Did you went?
✅ Did you go?

🌊 11. Past Continuous ▼

Past continuous describes an activity that was in progress at a particular time in the past.

S + WAS/WERE + V-ing

WAS

I / He / She / It

She was swimming.

WERE

You / We / They

They were snorkeling.
⚡ 12. When & While ▼

WHEN

Often connects an ongoing activity with another event.

I was snorkeling when I saw a turtle.

WHILE

Often connects activities happening during the same period.

I was swimming while my friend was taking pictures.
WHEN → event
WHILE → ongoing activity
